How it works

From a blank slate to a full cart, whenever you need it.

  1. 01

    Pick or create your meals

    Whenever you need an idea, allof suggests dinners shaped by your household's real tastes and dietary needs, each with a photo and a full recipe. Or describe a meal of your own and create it from scratch.

  2. 02

    Decide together

    Share ideas with the people you eat with and vote in seconds. Add the winners to your meal plan on the days you choose.

  3. 03

    A list that builds itself

    allof turns your meals into one organized grocery list, sorted by store section, and sends it straight to your Kroger cart. More retailers are on the way.

Rate what you cook and skip what doesn't land. allof adjusts its suggestions to your household over time.

Suggestions are the start. You're in control.

allof does the heavy lifting, and everything stays yours to change.

  • Make every recipe yours

    Swap an ingredient, scale the servings, ask for a variation, or start from your own idea. Customize recipes, quantities, ingredients and more.

  • Bring your recipes in

    Import recipes from recipe sites and social media posts by pasting a link or text. allof turns each one into your own version, with credit to the source.

  • Your own cookbooks

    Save favorites into custom cookbooks and build a collection that is genuinely yours.

  • Built for households

    Household accounts share more than the vote. Everyone sees the same plan, the same live grocery list, and the same recipes, so dinner stays coordinated.
